ABOUT THE DESTINATION

A Nation of 7,641 Islands

The Republic of the Philippines is a Southeast Asian archipelago nestled in the western Pacific Ocean. Known for pristine beaches, dramatic landscapes, and some of the warmest people on earth, the Philippines offers experiences found nowhere else.

From the powder-white sands of Boracay to the UNESCO rice terraces of Banaue, the emerald lagoons of Palawan to the surf breaks of Siargao — every island has its own story.

Quick Facts

  • 🗺 Capital: Manila
  • 🌎 Languages: Filipino (Tagalog) & English
  • 👥 Population: ~115 million
  • 🌴 Total Islands: 7,641
  • ⛅ Best Season: November – May (Dry Season)
  • 💲 Currency: Philippine Peso (PHP ₱)
  • ✈️ Airports: Manila (NAIA), Cebu (MCIA), Clark (CRK)

CULTURE & FLAVORS

Taste & Experience

Immerse yourself in the warmth, traditions, and food of Filipino life

🥩

Lechon

The king of Filipino celebrations — a whole roasted pig with crackling skin cooked over charcoal for hours. Cebu lechon is legendary worldwide.

🍲

Adobo

The unofficial national dish — chicken or pork braised in vinegar, soy sauce, and garlic. Every family has their own secret recipe.

🎉

Sinulog Festival

One of Asia's grandest festivals held in Cebu every January — vibrant street dancing, colorful costumes, and a solemn religious procession.

🍣

Halo-Halo

The ultimate Filipino dessert — shaved ice topped with ube, leche flan, sweetened beans, jellies, and evaporated milk in one colorful bowl.

🚍

Jeepney

The flamboyantly decorated icon of Philippine roads. Originally made from US military jeeps — now a symbol of Filipino creativity and culture.

🤝

Bayanihan Spirit

The uniquely Filipino spirit of communal unity. Visitors are welcomed like family — hospitality here isn't just a courtesy, it's a way of life.

BEFORE YOU GO

Travel Tips

Everything you need for a smooth and unforgettable Philippine adventure

01
📅

Best Time to Visit

Dry season runs November to May. December–February is the coolest and most comfortable. Avoid June–October (typhoon season) on the eastern coast.

02
✈️

Getting Around

Domestic flights link major islands quickly. Ferries connect the Visayas islands. Jeepneys, tricycles, and habal-habal are the local way to get around.

03
💵

Budget Guide

Budget travel: ₱1,500–₱2,500/day. Mid-range: ₱3,000–₱6,000/day. Always carry cash — many remote islands don't accept credit cards.

04
📱

Stay Connected

Buy a Globe or Smart SIM at the airport for affordable data. Wi-Fi is widely available in tourist areas. Signal can be weak on remote islands.

05
💊

Health & Safety

Drink bottled water only. Apply sunscreen and insect repellent daily. Most tourist areas are safe — keep an eye on belongings in busy markets and terminals.

06
🙏

Cultural Etiquette

Use "po" and "opo" when speaking to elders. Remove shoes before entering a home. Dress modestly when visiting churches. Tipping is appreciated but not required.
